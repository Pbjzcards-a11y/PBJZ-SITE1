01 — SENDING CARDS IN

How to submit

Four steps from your binder to my grading queue. Questions on any of it — message me before you ship.

1

Message me first

Reach out with what you're sending and the service level you want. I'll confirm pricing and turnaround before anything moves.

2

Pack them safe

Penny sleeve, then a top-loader or semi-rigid, taped so cards can't slide. Bundle in a team bag and pad the box well.

3

Ship to intake

Use a tracked, insured service. I'll send the intake address once we've confirmed your order over message.

4

Get your number

Once PSA logs the order I'll hand you a submission number — punch it into the tracker up top anytime for live status.
